Questions

Before you open an account

If the answer you need is not here, the support centre reaches a person rather than a form queue.

Contact support
What does it cost to hold an account?

Nothing. There is no account fee and no custody charge for holding a balance. The only fee is on a conversion, and it is quoted as a line of its own before you confirm — the example on this page shows exactly how it appears.

Which assets and currencies are supported?

Bitcoin, Ethereum and USDC on the asset side. Your portfolio can be priced and settled in 32 local currencies, and a payout account can be connected in each of them.

How long does identity verification take?

Usually one to two business days from the point your documents are uploaded. The status is shown in your dashboard the whole time, so you are never left guessing, and you can keep using the account for deposits while it runs.

Can I withdraw to an account that is not in my name?

No. A payout account has to be held in the same name as the HarbourX account. It is the single rule that keeps settlement clean, and it is what lets every later transfer to that account go through without a fresh review.

Is two-factor authentication required?

It is set up when you open the account and we strongly recommend leaving it on. HarbourX uses standard authenticator apps, and you are given backup codes at enrolment for the day your phone is not with you.

Is HarbourX giving me investment advice?

No. HarbourX holds, converts and settles the assets you already own. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and digital assets can lose value quickly — the risk of holding them stays with you.
