Before you write

The three things people ask most.

Why is my withdrawal still in review?

Bank withdrawals are released once identity verification is complete and the payout account has been confirmed. Check the Security page for your verification status — if it reads Verified and the request is still open after two business days, open a ticket with the reference from your transaction list.

I have lost my authenticator app.

Use one of the backup codes you saved when you turned on two-factor authentication — each signs you in once. If those are gone too, open a ticket from a device you have signed in on before, and do not share the codes with anyone who contacts you first.

Someone has asked me to pay a fee to release a withdrawal.

Stop. HarbourX never asks for an external payment to release a withdrawal, and never asks for your password or codes. Open a ticket under "Security concern" and tell us how they contacted you.
